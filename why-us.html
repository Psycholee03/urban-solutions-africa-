Why choose us

Your trusted municipal partner.

Urban Solutions Africa Pty Ltd is a specialist municipal rates consulting firm based in Gauteng, South Africa. We partner with property owners, developers, and conveyancers to navigate the complexities of municipal property rates, valuations, and billing.

Our Mission

To enable a flawless process of property registration by expediting the issuance of rates clearance figures and certificates, the creation of new accounts, and refund applications.

  • More than 8 years of municipal consulting experience
  • Deep operational knowledge of Johannesburg, Tshwane, and Ekurhuleni
  • Fast, efficient support for complex municipal queries
  • Ethical advice that protects our clients and our reputation

Our Approach

We scrutinise every line item, engage directly with municipal departments, and escalate matters through the correct legal channels when necessary. Our process is transparent, deadline-driven, and results-focused.
